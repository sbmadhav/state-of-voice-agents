Section 9: Health care notes.

For health systems, October stands and California adds a rule.

October's health care analysis stands: its published results, readiness by call type and regulation. One California law and three vendor reports are new.

California's AB 1979, new this month, was signed in October 2026 and takes effect on January 1, 2027. A licensed provider must exercise independent judgment when AI output informs care, AI may not perform reserved clinical functions, and health care chatbot operators are treated as providers under the state's medical privacy law. Scheduling and routine messages are exempt. Independent.

The vendor reports add detail to October's picture. A health plan administrator, new this month, is putting voice agents on inbound provider calls about eligibility, benefits, balances and claims status, handing off to people in its existing contact center (November 4). Vendor-reported. A vendor, new this month, reports that about 50 percent of calls are handled end to end in its most recently onboarded pilot clinics. Vendor-reported. NVIDIA reports that Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, new this month, cut its error rate on accented clinical speech from 30.8 percent to 23.8 percent with prompting alone, and to 11.8 percent after fine-tuning. Vendor-reported.

Those speech figures are the maker's own, so a health system should test on its own patients' audio. The TV-in-the-room finding applies equally to patients calling from home or a waiting room. Author's view.
