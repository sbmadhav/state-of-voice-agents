Section 4: Reliability.

PolyAI leads the benchmark, but its range overlaps Retell's.

Three platforms joined Cekura's benchmark this month and one of them, PolyAI, took first place from Retell, although the two platforms' ranges overlap.

Cekura's benchmark, which a testing vendor runs, puts each platform through 82 appointment and Medicare scenarios three times. It is a frozen release, so October's seven results stand and are in October, section 1; only the additions and status changes are here. The table sets each platform's repeat reliability, the share of scenarios passed on all three tries, beside its single-run score.

PolyAI now leads on repeat reliability, and every published single-run score is higher than the repeat score. Cekura reports all of the scores. Vendor-reported.

PolyAI passed 87.8 percent of scenarios on all three tries. Its single-run score is 98.8 percent. It is new and now ranks first. Its repetition and call-ending checks were not scored.

Telnyx passed 68.3 percent of scenarios on all three tries. Its single-run score is 97.6 percent. It is new and ranks fifth.

Smallest AI passed 45.1 percent of scenarios on all three tries. Its single-run score is not published. It is new. Under a modified tool check (its tools could not take list inputs), it scores 56.1 percent and 94.3 percent single-run.

Retell passed 75.6 percent of scenarios on all three tries. Its single-run score is 93.9 percent. Its scores are unchanged, but PolyAI's arrival moves it from first place to second.

GPT Realtime passed 64.6 percent of scenarios on all three tries. Its single-run score is 92.7 percent. It was unverified in October and is now listed, so it is verified.

With 82 scenarios, each score carries about plus or minus 7 to 10 points, so PolyAI's range (about 79 percent to 93 percent) overlaps Retell's (about 65 percent to 84 percent). The gap between one run and three holds everywhere: single-run scores beat repeat reliability on all ten configurations, by 18 points or more on nine. Vendor-reported.

Two other tests appeared this month. Retell, which sells a voice platform, published a second benchmark, new this month, from its production calls (27 models, 3,589 hallucination and tool-calling cases), and GPT-5.5 led at 84.8 percent free of hallucination with reasoning off. In Sierra's six-language voice test, new this month, Spanish trailed English by 2.4 points, so a bilingual bank should test both languages. Vendor-reported.
