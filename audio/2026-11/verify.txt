Section 10: Claim check.

Four claims October could not verify are now confirmed.

Four claims that October listed as unverified are now confirmed. The other 13 claims checked this month are new, and none of them is fully verified.

The four are the Decisions API's general availability, GPT Realtime's benchmark score, and the signing of California's AB 1609 and Colorado's replacement AI law. Several of the new claims fail in the same way: a maker names a capability and publishes no test. The other October verdicts stand, and are in October, T10. The buttons above the table filter it by verdict.

Of the 17 claims checked this month, four are verified, six are partly true or vendor-only, and seven are unverified or wrong. The verdicts are mine, and each row cites the source that decided it.

"OpenAI Decisions API is generally available." The verdict is verified. October listed this as unverified, because the API was in public beta. It became generally available on October 22, 2026, on GPT-6 Luna.

"GPT Realtime passes 64.63 percent of scenarios on all three tries (pass three)." The verdict is verified. October listed this as unverified. The score is now listed on the benchmark hub.

"California AB 1609 (customer service chatbots) was signed." The verdict is verified. October listed this as unverified. The bill was signed on September 28, 2026, as Chapter 733, and takes effect on January 1, 2027.

"Colorado's replacement AI law takes effect on January 1, 2027." The verdict is verified. October listed this as unverified. The law was signed on May 14, 2026, and takes effect on January 1, 2027, if rulemaking is complete.

"PolyAI leads Cekura Bench at 87.8 percent pass three." The verdict is partial or vendor. The score is on the hub, but two checks were not scored for PolyAI and its range overlaps the runner-up's.

"Jev can run locally or at the edge." The verdict is unverified or wrong. No weights or self-hosted build exist, and open Laya needs about 1 gigabyte, beyond a Cloudflare Worker's 128 megabytes.

"Banks are replacing contact-center staff with voice bots." The verdict is unverified or wrong. Commonwealth Bank cut 45 roles citing a voice bot, then reversed the cuts. No large US bank publishes voice-agent metrics.

"Voice biometrics is enough for high-risk banking calls." The verdict is unverified or wrong. New York's regulator advises against voice as an authentication factor, and detectors miss more over phone lines.

"Customers do not want AI on bank calls." The verdict is partial or vendor. About 30 percent accept it for routine tasks, and most want a person for fraud and collections.

"A TV in the room breaks most voice agents." The verdict is partial or vendor. In a vendor's research on simulated calls, task completion fell from 38.7 percent to 8.6 percent across 14 systems, with noise options off.

"An open model removes background voices better than commercial tools." The verdict is partial or vendor. VoxLock's word error rate was 4.95 percent, against 5.30 percent to 9.38 percent for three commercial tools, in its author's own test.

"Open-source speech recognition has caught up." The verdict is partial or vendor. Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe leads open models on recorded audio, but it does not stream and still trails the best closed models.

"Eleven Realtime starts speaking in 470 milliseconds." The verdict is unverified or wrong. Independent tests measured 0.57 seconds for the Max model and 0.62 seconds for the Mini. Its reasoning scores did match.

"Salesforce Launa-Omni matches the leading voice models." The verdict is partial or vendor. It is second on Scale's Audio MultiChallenge (59.69 against 60.40, within error), and its tau-cubed voice score is self-reported.

"Chatbase Voice gives speech-to-speech agents chat-grade control." The verdict is unverified or wrong. Chatbase names no model or architecture and publishes no tests.

"Fish Audio understands conversations, not just text." The verdict is unverified or wrong. Its models generate expressive multi-speaker speech from tagged scripts, and nothing shows reasoning over a live call.

"Block's Dessa is a usable full-duplex voice model." The verdict is unverified or wrong. Dessa is a research preview with a waitlist, and no size, benchmarks or tool use are published.

These verdicts describe the evidence as of November 14. What this update leaves untouched is indexed next.
