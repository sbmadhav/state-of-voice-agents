State of conversational voice agents. Monthly update. Financial services focus, with health care notes.

November 2026 edition.

Builds on the October 2026 baseline. Only what changed, or is reviewed for the first time, is here.

Banks and credit unions reach the front door.

Reviewed for the first time, bank and credit union voice-agent results look like October's health care results: routine calls such as balances, payments and branch hours, all reported by the institution or its vendor. A new entrant topped the reliability benchmark, OpenAI's decision API left beta, the FCC gave callers more flexibility on opt-outs, and California tightened rules on AI in health care. The new risk is ordinary: a TV playing in the room broke most voice agents in one test.

Best repeat reliability: 87.8 percent. October: 75.6 percent, 7 platforms. A new entrant, PolyAI, leads ten configurations; its range (about 79 percent to 93 percent) overlaps the runner-up's. Vendor-reported.

Share of all calls, dated bank result: 7 percent. October: not reviewed. Southern Cross Credit Union's voice agent takes 7 percent of all calls, mostly balances (October 27). Undated vendor case studies claim 37 percent to 90 percent. Vendor-reported.

Task completion, TV on: 8.6 percent. Same test, clean calls: 38.7 percent. Across 14 voice systems on simulated calls with a TV playing in the room. Vendor-reported.

TCPA opt-out rules: November 25. October: not reviewed. FCC 26-67 takes effect: one or more approved opt-out methods, disclosed on the call; category-limited opt-outs; a wider fraud-alert exemption. Independent.

Unchanged since October.

The three theses stand: reliability is the gap, think silently, then respond, and invest in governed memory. So do the economics model, the 90-day pilot and the router comparison. Everything else is indexed at the end with a link to its October section.

How to read this update: method, labels and disclosure.

Delta only. Findings published between October 12 and November 14, 2026 carry a "new" tag. Older material appears only if October did not cover it (most of the financial services evidence). Where a figure changed, the October value is shown next to it.

Labels. Independent. Vendor-reported. Company-reported. Author's view. Every figure links to its source.

Method. A review of public sources up to the research cutoff. I did not run my own tests.

Terms. "Repeat reliability" (pass three) is the share of test scenarios passed on all three tries. Word error rate is the share of words transcribed wrongly. Full-duplex models listen and talk at the same time. A System-1 model makes fast typed decisions (route, risk, done talking) without writing prose.
