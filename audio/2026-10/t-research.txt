T11. Tooling and implementation.

What to borrow, what to avoid.

Frontend and developer tooling.

Vercel's AI Voice Elements give React components for persona, mic capture, transcript and playback; useful for web prototypes, irrelevant to telephony.

Why implementations fail.

Abstract goals instead of concrete use cases, bots isolated from scheduling and CRM systems, no handoff path, messy knowledge, and no measurement after launch. Vendor-reported. OpenAI's own rebuild relied on a silent production test to find failures synthetic load tests missed.
