T3. Measuring reliability.

Single-run scores flatter every vendor.

An agent that completes a scenario once may fail it on the next call, and a single-run score hides that.

Repeat reliability, written pass k, counts a scenario only when all k of its runs succeed. The measure came from Sierra's tau-bench and now appears in Cekura Bench and in the voice mode of tau2-bench. Even if the runs are independent, small per-call error rates compound quickly. The slider below sets the per-call success rate, and the bars show how far pass two to pass five fall below it.

Each extra pass that a scenario must clear lowers the share of scenarios that succeed. The chart is arithmetic on the per-call rate, not a measurement of any product. Author's arithmetic.

Two findings complicate even this measure. The first is that a score depends on the test setup, or harness, and on the exact model. In Cekura's speech-to-speech benchmark (11 models, 2,952 calls), GPT Realtime 2.1 alone reached 79.3 percent pass three, while its mini sibling scored 50.0 percent. The mini model also saved all fields correctly on only 64.5 percent of data-saving calls, versus 92.0 percent. Vendor-reported.

The second is that recovery is its own axis. A 2026 interruption benchmark found task completion and recovery quality only partly related. Barge-in, when the caller interrupts the agent, and correction should therefore be tested separately from happy paths.

The release scorecard covers eight areas, and an exact match on tool-call arguments is the hard gate. The scorecard is my synthesis; its hard gate and release thresholds are taken from the sources cited.

Perception is measured by word error rate (the share of words transcribed wrongly) on phone-grade audio, by accent and language, and on noisy, multi-speaker recordings (a TV, a car, a family member talking). Entity accuracy is measured for names, dates, and phone and account numbers.

Routing is measured by intent accuracy, out-of-scope recall, the misroute rate (transfers after routing) and calibration error.

Turn-taking is measured by false and missed barge-ins and by backchannel handling.

Latency is measured as the 95th-percentile (P95) turn time, not the mean, and by dead air over 10 seconds.

Action correctness means an exact argument match on every tool call. This is the hard gate.

Repeatability means pass three on a golden set of 200 or more scenarios, with every production failure added as a regression case. A release is blocked if task completion drops more than 3 points or latency rises more than 10 percent.

Emotion detection is checked on labeled calls for frustration and confusion caught in real time, across accents and age groups. Sentiment is scored on every call after it ends.

Persona is tested on the same golden set: each persona passes it, and tone may differ but facts and policies may not.
