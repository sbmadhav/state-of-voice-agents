Section 10: The decision.

Four ways to play the next 12 months.

Buy a platform or vertical vendor Sierra, Hyro, Hippocratic AI.

Pros.

Fastest time to value.

Integrations and compliance contracts are standard.

Vendor carries tuning, safety layers and ops.

Cons.

Opaque pricing.

Less control over evaluation, routing and data.

Lock-in at the integration layer.

ROI logic: Abandonment and scheduling lift convert to revenue; labor avoided on in-scope queues. Verdict: Strong for the pilot itself.

Build on a platform Retell, LiveKit, Pipecat.

Pros.

Full transparency and model choice.

Lowest per-minute cost.

Own every trace and the router.

Cons.

Needs a dedicated team.

You own integration and safety.

Slower first launch.

ROI logic: About 8 cents to 14 cents a minute against roughly 6 dollars a human call; break-even depends on resolution rate. Verdict: Right later, not first.

Hybrid (recommended) Buy now, own the router and evals.

Pros.

Quick wins from a vertical vendor.

You build the test suite, routing layer, logs and governed fact store in parallel.

Builds the governed fact store, with the option to trial limited-memory models.

Keeps the exit door open.

Cons.

Two workstreams to govern.

Needs contract terms for log and eval export.

ROI logic: Captures near-term savings while building leverage for the next contract cycle. Verdict: Recommended.

Wait Revisit in 2027.

Pros.

No execution risk.

Models and benchmarks keep improving.

Cons.

Peers compound gains.

IVR frustration persists.

No internal evaluation muscle.

ROI logic: Negative: forgone abandonment and scheduling gains. Verdict: Not recommended.
